A beginner’s case-solving loop

You do not need to memorize every framework. Learn this six-step loop, then use the cards as specialized lenses. In an interview, explain your logic aloud and let evidence determine where you go deeper.

Step 1Clarify the decisionWhat must the client decide, by when, and how will success be measured?
Step 2Form a hypothesisState your early best guess. It guides priorities; it is allowed to change.
Step 3Build a structureSplit the problem into non-overlapping, testable branches tied to the objective.
Step 4Test the biggest branchAsk for data, calculate carefully, interpret the result, and update the hypothesis.
Step 5Create and compare optionsEvaluate value, feasibility, risk, timing, and reversibility—not just upside.
Step 6SynthesizeGive the answer first, then evidence, risks, mitigations, and immediate next steps.

Correlation guide

How the frameworks fit together

Most strong case structures use a sequence of lenses, not one giant framework.

01 · SCOPE

Clarify the decision

Use 3CPOR + SMART to pin down objective, context, constraints, company, product, competitors, and regulation.

02 · DIAGNOSE

Find the source

Use an equation or tree: profitability, volume–mix–price, issue tree, funnel, value chain.

03 · EXPLAIN

Understand why

Apply 5 Whys, 3Cs, customer journey, Porter, PESTLE, VRIO only where the diagnosis points.

04 · CHOOSE

Prioritize action

Compare options with decision matrix, NPV, scenarios, risk–impact, then define owners and milestones.
